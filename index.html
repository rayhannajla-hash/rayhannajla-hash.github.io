About

Thirty years of operations, now pointed at software

I'm Budhiarso Santosa, based in Cikarang, Indonesia, working remote. I spent roughly three decades running production floors for Japanese manufacturers — Sanyo and Yanmar among them — where a process that fails at 3 a.m. is a real problem with real consequences.

That's the mindset I bring to this work. A deck is a process for moving someone from skeptical to convinced. An automation is a process that has to survive without anyone watching it. Both reward the same thing: clear structure, and a reliability-first habit of asking what happens when it breaks.

I'm self-taught on the software side and I build in public — I run Fakta Psikologi, an Indonesian psychology channel on YouTube and Instagram, on top of a content pipeline I built and maintain myself. It's the honest version of a portfolio: a system I use every day, not a case study.
