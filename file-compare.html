About this demo

How the matching actually works

Two signals, not one

Header names are compared on wording, known equivalents (qty = quantity, DOB = date of birth) and spelling distance. Then the column's contents are profiled — emails, dates, numbers, value overlap — so a column can be matched even when its name gives nothing away. Pairings are one-to-one and assigned best-first.

Nothing is uploaded

Parsing, matching, comparison and the Excel export all happen in this page. Your files never reach a server — which is usually the deciding factor when the data is customer or payroll records. A server-side version with saved jobs and an audit trail is a straightforward extension.
