How it works

Four moving parts, none of them yours

  1. It pulls the data on a schedule. A scheduled job wakes up at whatever time you pick and calls each platform's official API directly — YouTube Data and Analytics, Instagram Graph, Google Analytics. No scraping, no browser sitting open, no third-party service holding your tokens.
  2. It compares against last week. Raw numbers are close to useless on their own. Every metric is stored and shown against the previous period, so you see direction, not just size.
  3. An AI model reads the change and writes the short version. Two or three plain-language notes on what actually shifted and the likeliest reason — the part you'd normally work out yourself while squinting at a graph.
  4. It delivers, then goes back to sleep. The report arrives as a normal chat message in Telegram or Slack. If a platform's API is down or a token expires, it retries, and tells you rather than failing silently.
